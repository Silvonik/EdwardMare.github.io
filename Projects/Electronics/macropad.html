Macropad

A custom made Macropad with useful shortcuts for the keys, like copy or undo. (STILL BUILDING PROJECT)

Kicad | Fusion360 | C++

About

The Macropad is a custom macropad to help me with everyday repetitive keyboard shorcuts.

The Macropad is still being made but I finished the designing stage, to design the Macropad I had to make a Schematic, PCB(Printed Circut Board), A 3D Printed Case and the firmware.

I used Fusion 360 for the 3D Printed case, Arduino IDE for the firmware, Kicad to make the Schematic and the PCB.

I am now waiting for all of the parts to arrive so I can build the Macropad!
