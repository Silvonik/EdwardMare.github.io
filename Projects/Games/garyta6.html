Gary's Theft Auto 6

A GTA 6 inspired crime game featuring NPCs, police and chaos.

Unity | 3D | C#

About

Gary's Theft Auto 6 is A GTA style game I made in only 7 days featuring an awesome playable character called "Gary" who has no main goal but to cause chaos.

I made the game from scratch and had to learn new stuff like Pathfinding for the Police's AI system.

I was very happy with the finished result of Gary's Theft Auto 6. During development, I didn't think the game would turn out this well, as I only had a week to make it while still going to school
