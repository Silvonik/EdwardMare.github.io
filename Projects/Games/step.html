Step By Step

A 2D Platformer with 18 Levels.

Unity | 2D | C#

About

In Step By Step you play as the character Tim, and you have to help Tim complete 18 levels through the caves of Idinland.

The game was made when I was still a beginner so I learnt a lot of stuff like levels, scene changing, 2D player movement and many other stuff.

The game was great when I released it and all of my friends who played it did speedruns through the game and they all tried to beat eachothers time, which made me add a speedrun clock on the screen in version 2.
